Frequently Asked Questions

What is QRHub?

QRHub is a professional, client-side QR Code studio that allows anyone to generate, customize, and bulk-generate print-ready QR codes for 17 specialized data types completely free.

Does QRHub store my QR code data?

No. QRHub operates 100% within your client browser. Your URLs, text, Wi-Fi credentials, and contacts are converted to QR matrices locally and never transmitted to any cloud servers.

Which export formats are supported?

We support lossless vector SVG and PDF (for infinite sharpness on posters and billboards), print presets (table tents, business cards, stickers), as well as raster PNG, JPG, and WebP.

Can I bulk-generate QR codes?

Yes. QRHub includes a Bulk Generator capable of processing up to 200 QR codes from CSV or multi-line lists with one-click vector and zip download.
